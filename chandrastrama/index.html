FAQ

What is Chandrashtama?

Chandrashtama occurs when the Moon transits the 8th house from your natal Moon sign (Rasi). It usually lasts for about 2.25 days every month.

Why is it considered vulnerable?

The 8th house represents obstacles and sudden changes. When the mind (Moon) passes through this house, one may feel stressed or prone to misunderstandings.
