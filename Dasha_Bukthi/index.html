Understanding Dasa & Bhukti

Dasa represents the broad, macro-themes of your life based on planetary cycles, while Bhukti reveals the specific, short-term chapters within it. Together, these celestial blueprints help pinpoint the exact timing of significant events across your health, wealth, career, and relationships.

By understanding your current planetary periods, you can make informed decisions and align your actions with the natural cosmic rhythm, maximizing opportunities and mitigating challenges.

✦ Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dasa?▼
Dasa is a major planetary period in Vedic Astrology that unfolds based on the Moon’s Nakshatra position at birth.
What is Bhukti?▼
Bhukti is a sub-period within a major Dasa that specifies focused events over 1 to 3 years.
How are planetary periods calculated?▼
Calculations follow the 120-year Vimshottari Dasa cycle starting from your birth Nakshatra degree.
